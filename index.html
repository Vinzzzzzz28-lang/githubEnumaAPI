04 — About

The person behind the pull requests

I'm a frontend developer who spent the last six years turning slow, confusing interfaces into fast, obvious ones — and I care as much about how a team works as I do about the final pixel.

My path started in design, drifted into front-end engineering, and settled somewhere in between: I still sketch before I ship, but I measure everything after. I specialise in React and TypeScript codebases that need to scale without losing their personality, and in design systems that survive contact with real product teams.

When I'm not profiling a bundle, you'll find me writing onboarding docs for new engineers or arguing (politely) that a 90 kB JavaScript budget is a perfectly reasonable thing to enforce in CI.
